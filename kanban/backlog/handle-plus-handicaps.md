# Plus handicaps

## Context

In golf, when a handicap goes below zero it is consider a 'plus' handicap , e.g. +1 is actually one stroke beyond 0 (-1) but is called 'plus 1'. It involves giving strokes back to the course or competitors rather than being given strokes.

## What

This ticket is to review the use of handicaps throughout the app to better handle plus handicaps, both logically/function wise and presentationally.

Rather than use negative numbers to capture it, I would prefer a checkbox that prepends the figure with a '+'

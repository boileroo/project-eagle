# Enable teams

## What

The Teams sub-panel and the 'Enable Teams' toggle

## Context

Make the 'Enable Teams' panel more engaging and clear.

Update the styling to be a little more interesting that a simple small toggle.

Add some helper text explaining what 'teams' are and what it enables (e.g. matches between teams for points and a team standing in addition to individual).

Also mention how teams are fixed and are different to 'groupings' which are merely a way of grouping players in rounds. Teams span multiple rounds.

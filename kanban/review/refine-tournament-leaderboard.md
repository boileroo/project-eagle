# Tournament leaderboard

## Context

For tournaments (not single rounds) the leaderboard section needs work. We need to make it clearer who is winning each of the individual or team competitions, and what the scores are/were.

## What

- one table showing the individual scores across each round, inc. gross, nett, stableford, and bonuses for each round, plus cumulatively. For now just show all columns, no filtering or sorting required. The table should sort by default by total stableford points. Making the table better will be a future task.
- one section showing the winner(s) of any 'games' played between individuals in each round, if applicable, OR one section showing the team matches and the outcomes and the overall team leaderboard

Really think about this and how the results of each round all aggregate together (or not) and what needs displaying. Consider the UX first and foremost both in terms of information clarity, but also useability and configurability.
